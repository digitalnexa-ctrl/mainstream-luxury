Everything you need to know about our atelier, fabrics, sizing, and shipping.

How do MAINSTREAM silhouettes fit?

Our garments use a relaxed luxury-streetwear drape. T-shirts feature dropped shoulders and a boxy torso; hoodies have a structured hood and relaxed chest. Size down for a more tailored fit.

What makes MAINSTREAM Indian cotton special?

We focus on long-staple Indian cotton and heavyweight constructions selected for hand-feel, structure and durability.

How long does delivery take?

Domestic delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Remote areas may require additional time.

Can I pay via COD or UPI?

Yes. Checkout can support UPI, cards, net banking and Cash on Delivery when enabled for the order.
